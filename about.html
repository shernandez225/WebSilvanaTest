What is Artemis?

Artemis is NASA’s lunar exploration program designed to return humans to the Moon and establish a long-term presence for future deep space missions.

Mission Technology

The Artemis missions combine advanced spacecraft systems, lunar landing technology, and next-generation engineering to support sustainable human exploration.

Mission FAQ

The Artemis program aims to return astronauts to the Moon and prepare humanity for future missions to Mars.

Lunar exploration helps scientists develop technologies, research deep space environments, and understand how humans can live beyond Earth.

NASA collaborates with international agencies, engineers, scientists, and private aerospace companies as part of the Artemis missions.
